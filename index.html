Friday · TDJ

Juampi

Juan Pablo Canavire · Argentina / Sweden

Juan Pablo Canavire, known as DJ Juampi, brings the authentic spirit of Argentine tango to every dance floor.

Originally from Jujuy, Argentina, he developed his career in Buenos Aires, spending six years as resident DJ at DNI Tango. Those years shaped his musical identity and understanding of the dance floor.

Now based in Stockholm, Juampi plays internationally. Drawing on recordings by the orchestras of tango’s Golden Age, his tandas blend rhythm, emotion and dynamics to energize the dance floor and connect with the dancers.

Saturday · TDJ

Mariko

Ukraine / Israel

DJ Mariko is a tango DJ and professional sound engineer.

A tango DJ since 2012, she has played at milongas and tango events in various countries. Her background in sound engineering informs her close attention to sound quality, balance and detail.

For Mariko, every night is a story created in the moment — through music, the energy of the dance floor and her connection with the dancers. Her sets have a natural flow and dramatic arc that unfold throughout the evening.
